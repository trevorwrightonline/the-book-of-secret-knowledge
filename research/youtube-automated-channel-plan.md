# AI-Agent-Run YouTube Channel: Niche Research & 0→1,000 Subscriber Plan

_Researched October 2026._

## TL;DR

- **Niche:** Data-driven personal finance and economics explainers. Working concept: **"Money, in Charts."** Each video answers one money question that ordinary people care about, using real public data turned into animated charts. Examples: "What a $100k salary is actually worth in every US state", "How long it takes to save for a house, by city", "Is renting really throwing money away? We ran 40 years of numbers."
- **Why:** It pays some of the highest CPMs on YouTube (finance and investing are commonly quoted at roughly $12–$45 CPM), it stays relevant for years, people search for it, and short charts work well as Shorts. Most importantly, AI agents do this work well: fetching data, analysing it, and rendering charts in code. That gives every video original analysis, which is what keeps an automated channel clear of YouTube's "inauthentic content" enforcement.
- **Path to 1,000 subs:** Post a lot of Shorts to get reach (2–3 per day). Add 2 long-form videos per week to turn viewers into subscribers and build watch hours. Each Short points to a related long-form video. Review analytics weekly and drop formats that don't work.
- **Realistic target:** 1,000 subscribers in **60–120 days** if you keep up the output and the quality. Nobody can guarantee it. The plan below is built to give it the best odds.
- **Deadline to know about:** YouTube has announced that from **1 Feb 2027**, new Partner Program applicants will need **8,000 watch hours or 20M Shorts views** (currently 4,000 hours or 10M Shorts views). The 1,000-subscriber minimum stays the same. Today is about 15 weeks before that date. Push hard now, but qualifying before February is a stretch goal, not something to count on.

## 1. Ground rules that shape everything

### 1.1 "Fully automated" vs. YouTube policy
In July 2025, YouTube renamed its "repetitious content" policy to **"inauthentic content."** It covers *mass-produced or repetitive content*: videos that look template-made with little variation, or that could easily be copied at scale. In January 2026 YouTube terminated a group of large AI "slop" channels, and creators report that enforcement now looks at the **whole channel**, not single videos. Using AI is **not** banned in itself. Faceless channels are not being demonetized as a category.

What this means for the design:
1. **Every video needs something original.** Here that is original data analysis, so the niche choice itself is the main protection.
2. **Vary the format.** Use several video templates, rotate hooks and structures, and avoid one-template-forever.
3. **Keep a human gate.** Agents produce everything, but you approve each upload, about 2 minutes per Short and 10 minutes per long-form. This also protects you from finance mistakes. Plan on **30–45 minutes a day**. Treat "zero human" as a goal for later, once the channel is monetized and stable.
4. **Disclose AI** using YouTube's altered/synthetic-content setting whenever a video contains realistic synthetic material. Animated charts with narration usually don't need it, but a cloned human voice or realistic AI footage does.

### 1.2 Things that will get the channel killed (never do)
- Buying subscribers or views, sub-for-sub schemes, view bots. These break the rules, and they also wreck the engagement signals the algorithm relies on.
- Re-uploading or lightly editing other people's content, or reading Reddit posts and articles aloud.
- Faking data or making specific investment calls ("buy X now"). Finance is a "Your Money or Your Life" (YMYL) topic. Mistakes cost you trust, and for some claims they can create legal exposure.
- Fake engagement from agent-run accounts, such as comments or likes from alt channels.

### 1.3 Monetization thresholds (Oct 2026)
| Tier | Requirement | Unlocks |
|---|---|---|
| YPP tier 1 | 500 subs + 3,000 watch hrs (12 mo) **or** 3M Shorts views (90 d) | Memberships, Super Thanks, Shopping |
| YPP tier 2 (now) | 1,000 subs + 4,000 watch hrs **or** 10M Shorts views | Ad revenue |
| YPP tier 2 (from 1 Feb 2027, new applicants) | 1,000 subs + 8,000 watch hrs **or** 20M Shorts views | Ad revenue |

Watch hours and Shorts views count as **separate** paths and don't combine. Shorts bring subscribers fast, but long-form produces the watch hours. That is why the plan uses both.

## 2. Niche research

### 2.1 How the candidates were scored
Each niche got a 1–5 score on six criteria:
- **$ (RPM):** ad pay
- **Evergreen:** whether old videos keep earning
- **Shorts fit:** works for growth
- **Agent fit:** can AI make it *well* and *originally*
- **Policy risk:** inverted, so higher means safer
- **Side income:** affiliate, sponsor and product potential

| Niche | $ | Evergreen | Shorts fit | Agent fit | Policy safety | Side income | **Total** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Personal finance / economics, data-driven** | 5 | 5 | 4 | 5 | 4 | 5 | **28** |
| AI tools & software tutorials | 3 | 2 (tools change fast) | 3 | 5 | 4 | 5 | 22 |
| Cybersecurity explainers | 5 | 4 | 3 | 3 | 4 | 4 | 23 |
| Insurance / legal / B2B SaaS | 5 | 4 | 2 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 20 |
| History / geography documentaries | 2 | 5 | 4 | 4 | 3 | 2 | 20 |
| Science / "how it works" | 2 | 5 | 4 | 4 | 4 | 2 | 21 |
| Reddit stories / horror narration / compilations | 1 | 2 | 5 | 5 | **1** | 1 | 15 |
| Kids content | 1 | 4 | 3 | 4 | 1 (COPPA, few ads) | 1 | 14 |

### 2.2 Why data-driven finance wins
- **Money:** It is in the top CPM group. Advertisers include banks, brokerages, fintech, insurers and tax software. Q4 CPMs typically run **30–50% higher** than Q1, and we are launching into Q4.
- **Side income beyond AdSense:** Finance affiliate programs (brokerages, budgeting apps, high-yield savings accounts) pay some of the highest per-signup fees anywhere. Agents can also build digital products such as budget spreadsheets and calculators, plus a newsletter.
- **Engagement:** "Where does my state rank?" and "Is this true for me?" make people comment, which feeds the algorithm. Comparisons by state, city, age or salary bring endless variety without repeating a template.
- **Defensibility:** Generic "5 money tips" channels are saturated and exactly the kind of slop that gets demonetized. A channel whose every video comes from **a fresh data analysis** (BLS, Census ACS, FRED, Zillow research data, BEA, OECD) is hard to copy and plainly original.
- **Agent strengths:** Fetching datasets, running pandas analyses and rendering charts in code is reliable, testable and cheap. It is far more dependable than generating AI stock footage.

**Runner-up:** AI tools & software tutorials. Agents can test tools and record their screens themselves, but the content goes stale quickly and the CPM is lower. A good second channel once the first is monetized.

### 2.3 Positioning
- **Audience:** US adults aged 22–45, the highest-CPM geography and demographic. Publish in English.
- **Promise:** "The real numbers behind your money decisions. No hype, no stock picks."
- **Tone:** Calm, curious, a little contrarian. Every claim is sourced and every chart cites its dataset on screen.
- **Name ideas:** _Money, in Charts_ · _The Real Numbers_ · _Plotted Money_ · _Data Dollar_. Check handle availability and trademarks before choosing.

## 3. Content system

### 3.1 Formats (rotate; never fewer than 4 in use)
| Format | Length | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Ranking map** (Short) | 25–45 s | "Every state ranked by how far $75k goes" |
| **One shocking chart** (Short) | 15–30 s | "Rent vs wages since 1985" |
| **Myth test** (Short / long) | 40 s / 8–12 min | "Is buying always better than renting? We tested 40 years." |
| **Deep dive** (long) | 10–15 min | "The true cost of a car, decade by decade" |
| **Calculator story** (long) | 8–12 min | "What if you'd invested your coffee money since 2000?" |
| **Monthly data drop** (long, recurring) | 6–10 min | "Inflation/jobs/housing: what changed this month" (timely, high search interest) |

### 3.2 Cadence for the first 90 days
- **Shorts:** 2 per day, rising to 3 per day after week 3 if quality holds. Most of the subscriber volume comes from here.
- **Long-form:** 2 per week. Produces watch hours and higher-value subscribers.
- **Recycling:** Each long-form video yields 3–5 Shorts. Each Short links to a related long-form video.
- **Community tab posts:** 3 per week (polls like "Guess the #1 state"), once the feature is available.

### 3.3 Quality bar (agents must pass before human review)
- Hook in the first 1.5 s. Shorts: the claim plus the chart already moving. Long-form: the payoff question in the first 10 s.
- Every number traces back to a source URL stored in a `sources.json` file with each video. Show the citation on screen.
- No section of a long-form video goes more than about 20 s without a new visual.
- The title promises one specific, checkable thing. The thumbnail shows one chart or map and at most 3 words.
- A fact-check agent independently recomputes key numbers from the raw data and blocks the upload on any mismatch.

**Stack (examples; swap freely):**
- **LLM agents:** Claude (latest models) via the Agent SDK. Run the orchestrator on a scheduler such as cron, GitHub Actions or a small VPS.
- **Data:** FRED API, BLS API, Census API, Zillow/Redfin research CSVs, BEA, World Bank/OECD.
- **Visuals:** Remotion (React → video) or Manim for animated charts, with matplotlib/Plotly for static frames. Use stock footage only as accent. Charts rendered from code are original, consistent and cheap.
- **Voice:** A high-quality TTS voice under a commercial licence (ElevenLabs or similar). Pick **one** consistent voice; this voice is the channel's identity. Don't clone a real person.
- **Assembly:** FFmpeg. Captions via Whisper-style transcription burned into Shorts.
- **Upload:** YouTube Data API v3. The default quota is 10,000 units/day and an upload costs about 1,600 units, so about 6 uploads/day by default. Check the current figures and request more quota if needed.
- **Thumbnails:** Code-generated from the chart plus bold text. Use YouTube's built-in "Test & Compare" to A/B test up to 3 thumbnails on long-form.
- **Budget:** About $100–$300/month (LLM API, TTS, stock/music licence, VPS). No paid ads needed.

## 5. Execution timeline

### Week 0: setup (3–5 days)
- [ ] Pick the name, then reserve the handle and matching domain, email, X/Threads/TikTok/Instagram handles.
- [ ] Create a **Brand Account** channel, turn on 2-Step Verification, verify the phone number (unlocks long uploads and custom thumbnails), set the country to US.
- [ ] Brand kit: logo, banner, 3 thumbnail templates, chart colour palette, intro sting (≤1 s), consistent font.
- [ ] Build the pipeline end-to-end on **one** test video. Don't scale until a test video passes the quality bar.
- [ ] Build a backlog of **20 Shorts + 4 long-form** before launch so the cadence never breaks.
- [ ] Channel trailer (45 s) + "start here" playlist + clear channel description with keywords.

### Weeks 1–4: launch and find out what works
- 2 Shorts/day + 2 long-form/week, scheduled at consistent times (US evenings, about 6–9 pm ET; adjust once Analytics shows when your audience is online).
- Test **all six formats** and **3+ topic clusters**: housing, salaries/cost of living, retirement/investing basics, inflation/prices, debt/cars.
- Every Short ends with a spoken + on-screen "Full breakdown in the linked video" and uses the "Related video" link field.
- Every long-form video gets a pinned comment with a question and links in the description to sources and the related playlist.
- Reply to **every** comment within 24 h for the first 90 days. This is the easiest engagement lever early on.
- **Weekly review (Analyst agent → you):** Shorts "viewed vs swiped away" %, average % viewed, subs per 1k views, long-form CTR and 30-second retention.

### Weeks 5–8: double down
- Cut the bottom 50% of formats and topics by subs-per-1k-views. Make 3–5 variations of every outlier (any video at more than 3× the channel median).
- Raise Shorts to 3/day if retention holds.
- Start the **monthly data drop** series (timely, searchable, a reason to subscribe).
- Collaborations: reach out to 5–10 small finance creators (1k–50k subs) offering custom chart packages or co-analysis in exchange for a shout-out or collab video. This is the fastest non-algorithmic subscriber source.
- Seed (don't spam) relevant communities: share charts with useful context in r/dataisbeautiful, r/personalfinance, finance X/Threads. Follow each community's self-promotion rules.

### Weeks 9–12+: to 1,000 subscribers, then monetization
- Expected milestone range: **1,000 subscribers between about day 60 and day 120.** Shorts-driven channels in strong niches often get there sooner. Long-form-only channels usually take longer.
- Once at 500 subs + 3k hours or 3M Shorts views, apply for YPP tier 1 (memberships etc.).
- Turn on side income as soon as allowed: affiliate links (with FTC disclosure: "affiliate links, I may earn a commission"), newsletter capture, a $9–$19 "personal finance spreadsheet kit" built by agents.
- Long-form watch hours: aim for at least 2 deep dives a week over 10 minutes with 40%+ retention. Each pinned to the channel and added to binge-able playlists.

## 6. KPIs and kill/pivot rules

| Metric | Target by day 30 | Day 60 | Day 90 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Subscribers | 100–300 | 400–700 | 1,000+ |
| Shorts avg views | 1k+ | 3k+ | 5k+ |
| Shorts viewed (vs swiped) | > 60% | > 70% | > 70% |
| Long-form CTR | > 4% | > 5% | > 6% |
| Long-form avg view duration | > 35% | > 40% | > 45% |
| Subs per 1k Shorts views | > 1 | > 2 | > 2 |

- **Day 30 checkpoint:** If Shorts average under 500 views and no single video has broken out, change the hook style and topic cluster, not the niche.
- **Day 60 checkpoint:** Under 300 subs → sharpen the angle within finance (e.g. "housing data only" or "salary data only"). A narrower channel is easier for the algorithm to place.
- **Never** respond to slow growth by cutting quality or posting near-duplicates. That is exactly what the inauthentic-content enforcement catches.

## 7. Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Inauthentic-content demonetization | Original analysis in every video, rotating formats, human approval, AI disclosure where needed |
| Factual error in finance content | Fact-check agent recomputes numbers, sources on screen, "educational, not financial advice" note, no stock picks |
| API / tool changes | Keep the pipeline modular (any TTS/LLM swappable), store all source assets in git/object storage |
| YPP bar doubling on 1 Feb 2027 | Front-load output now; plan income from affiliate + products, which don't depend on YPP |
| Algorithm dry spells | Collab and community distribution, newsletter list as an owned audience |
| Copyright | Only public data plus licensed music, voice and stock; no third-party clips |
